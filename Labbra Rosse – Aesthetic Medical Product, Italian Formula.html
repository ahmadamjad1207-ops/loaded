Questions, answered.

Who can use Labbra Rosse?

Only authorised medical professionals trained in perioral aesthetic injection techniques.

What is the difference between L and W?

L is red-tinted for immediate gloss and colour vitality, the rouge effect. W is a pure clear gel for contouring and structure. Both use the same modified HA matrix.

Which needles are suitable?

The gel is designed for smooth delivery through 27G and 30G fine needles.

How should it be stored?

Between 2 °C and 25 °C in a dry place, protected from direct sunlight and freezing.

Can a syringe be shared or reused?

No. Each syringe is for a single patient and single use. Do not resterilise, and follow standard aseptic technique.

Is it CE marked?

Yes. The leaflet lists CE 0425 and Italian formula (EU origin).
